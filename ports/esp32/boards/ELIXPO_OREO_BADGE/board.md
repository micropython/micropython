The following files are firmware for the Oreo Badge, an open-hardware
conference badge built around an ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N16R8 module (16MiB flash,
8MiB octal SPIRAM) with a 2.0" 320x240 ST7789 display, MPU-6050 IMU, infrared
receiver and Li-ion charging over USB-C.

The design files (CERN-OHL-P-2.0) and OreoOS, the MicroPython-based software
the badge ships with, are at https://github.com/elixpo/oreo.
